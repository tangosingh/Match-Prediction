Under the Hood
HOW IT WORKS
01
Feature Engineering
Raw inputs are encoded, scaled with RobustScaler, and combined into interaction features like batting × powerplay.
02
4 Models Vote
Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost each independently predict Win or Loss.
03
Ensemble Decision
Weighted average of all 4 model probabilities. Random Forest and XGBoost carry higher weight due to better F1 scores.
04
Confidence Score
The final probability is shown as confidence. Above 60% is a strong signal. Below 55% indicates a close match.
